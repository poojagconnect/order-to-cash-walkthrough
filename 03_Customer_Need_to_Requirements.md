# Customer Need to Solution Requirements

Part of presales is listening to what a customer says and turning it into requirements the solution team can use. These are three made-up examples to practise.

## Example 1: A faster plan launch
**Customer says:** "Our marketing team wants to launch a festival offer next week, but IT says it will take two months."

| System | Requirement |
|--------|-------------|
| Product catalog | Business users can create and edit offers, prices and dates without code |
| Order management | New offer is available in all sales channels at the same time |
| Charging | New offer rules apply from the start date without a system change |
| Billing | Offer shows correctly on the bill |

**Question to ask:** Who approves a new offer today, and how long does each approval take?

## Example 2: Customers call about wrong bills
**Customer says:** "We get many calls from customers saying their bill is higher than expected."

| System | Requirement |
|--------|-------------|
| Charging | Usage is counted once, and extra usage charges follow the plan rules |
| Billing | Bill shows plan fee, extra usage and tax on separate lines |
| CRM | Support agents see the customer's bill and usage in one screen |
| Billing | Dispute workflow with approval and credit notes |

**Question to ask:** What are the top three reasons customers give when they call?

## Example 3: One customer, many services
**Customer says:** "Many of our customers have mobile and broadband with us, but our teams cannot see both."

| System | Requirement |
|--------|-------------|
| CRM | One customer profile linked to all of their services |
| Billing | Option for one combined bill |
| Product catalog | Bundles that include mobile and broadband |
| Order management | One order can include more than one service |

**Question to ask:** Today, how many separate systems hold a customer's data?
